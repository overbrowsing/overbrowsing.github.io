    Practice-Led Initiatives

    At Overbrowsing, we approach part of our research through a practice-led methodology, blending academic inquiry with real-world applications. Our open-source projects are shaped by the insights we gain from exploring the environmental impact of digital practices, and our aim is to develop actionable solutions that respond directly to the challenges we uncover. Our projects are designed to evolve based on what we learn, testing theories and refining solutions as we go. We don't just theorise about sustainability on the web; we actively engage with these issues to create tools and resources that drive meaningful change.
