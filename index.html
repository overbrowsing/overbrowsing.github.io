    Exploring the web of yesterday, today and tomorrow

    An applied research initiative investigating the evolution, impact and sustainability of the web.

    As seen in

    404: Emissions not found

    Overbrowsing investigates how the web changes over time and what those changes reveal about its environmental impact. Using web archives as a historical record of the evolving internet, we develop methods for measuring digital growth, understanding long-term trends and informing more sustainable approaches to the design and stewardship of the web.
